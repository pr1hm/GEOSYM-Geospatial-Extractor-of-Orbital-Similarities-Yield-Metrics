### Why the Name Fits Like an Extra-Vehicular Spacesuit
"Planetary Analog Explorer" was a functional working title, but it didn't do justice to the sheer mathematical firepower under the hood. 

What we actually built is an end-to-end vector pipeline:
- **Terrestrial:** 63 barren Earth sites (15 NASA historical anchors + 48 newly screened discoveries) streamed via windowed 5x5 km COG footprints directly from Microsoft Planetary Computer STAC.
- **Analog:** Direct cross-planetary physical calibration against Lunar South Pole (Shackleton, Malapert) and Martian delta/paleolake systems (Jezero, Mawrth, Arcadia).
- **Vector:** The 8-dimensional normalized physical telemetry vector (mean slope, TRI ruggedness, SAR roughness dB, radar depolarization ratio, diurnal $\Delta T$, cryogenic floor, ferric iron oxide index, and basaltic mafic index) coupled with the 64-dimensional latent terrain morphology embedding generated by `ibm-nasa-geospatial/Prithvi-EO-2.0-600M`.
- **Matching Engine:** Sub-5ms client-side weighted cosine similarity kernel with real-time dynamic re-weighting across 5 operational mission presets and live Groq LLM protocol synthesis.

**TAVME** captures both the scientific rigor and the algorithmic core of the challenge.
